# Scenario-Set Review (20-canonical / CM24) and R6 Agenda — 2026-08-31

Verdict: intelligent in construction, largely complete for *ranking allocations* inside the current equity+alternatives universe, but not yet wise about its own fallibility — the left tail is compressed, the set spans fewer independent futures than its count suggests, and one asset class the missing scenarios would reward (duration) cannot be priced by the deck at all.

## What is genuinely good
- Breadth and currency: fiscal dominance, shadow-credit accident, both AI sides (boom and capex disappointment), kinetic Taiwan, cyber systemic, energy shock, JGB/duration liquidity, climate transition and physical, governance shock.
- Two-sided thematic risk, including vehicle risk (CM09 "right thesis, wrong vehicle") and margin risk (CM16 defense procurement squeeze) — rare in practice.
- Auditable: CM24 derivations reproduce exactly from the documented crosswalk (validated to 0.000); robust 50/25/25 blending guards weight mis-specification.

## Measured weaknesses
1. Effective dimensionality ≈ 2. PCA across the 75-ticker deck: factor 1 = 76% of cross-scenario variance, top-2 = 94%, participation ratio 1.6, only 2 eigenvalues > 1 (no near-duplicate pairs, corr < 0.985). The scenarios mostly agree on ticker ordering; their disagreement — what actually drives allocation — lives on roughly two axes (growth-beta vs real-asset/inflation, plus kinetic-tail severity). 24 labels ≠ 24 independent futures.
2. Compressed left tail. Across all 24 scenarios no portfolio lands below ~5.5% net CAGR; history offers 40-year windows for concentrated equity far worse. The set stress-tests allocation, not survival. Standing sensitivity now computed: all rows −200bp ⇒ P1 geoCE 10.7→2.5, P5(rob-G) 0.05, ruin 4.5%; R2_final 13.4→3.7, P5 0.21. Ordering fully preserved (optimized books degrade least) — absolute comfort (e.g., "5.7x at worst", DRB40@6=0) is conditional on level calibration.
3. Point estimates without uncertainty grades. Row-level model risk (QTUM, URNM, CEG episodes) had to be patched externally via haircuts; the set itself carries no confidence column beyond Source.
4. Coverage gaps: no deflationary bust / balance-sheet-recession regime (only asset that hedges it, duration, has one IEF row — TLT/ZROZ/GOVZ absent from deck, so even adding the scenario cannot reward the hedge until rows exist); no USD/reserve-institutional crisis distinct from JGB liquidity (gold's insurance role under-priced); no broad kinetic conflict beyond Taiwan-semis; no AI-abundance disinflation or AI-driven social/tax rupture. CM24 also silently drops three source scenarios (Cronyism direct, US-regional credit accident, Pandemic/Bio) — it is not a superset of the 20.
5. Lumpy canonical weights: Base+Reflation = 26% of mass; climate legs 1.5% each. Robust blend mitigates but levels of PathCE inherit the optimism.
6. Event/regime conflation: episodic shocks (cyber event, credit accident) encoded as 40-year CAGRs; λ=1/8 Markov mixing partially compensates.

## R6 agenda (concrete)
1. Add four scenarios: Deflationary bust (Japan-style), USD/reserve-institutional crisis (repression with negative real financial-asset returns; gold/hard assets rewarded), Broad kinetic conflict (beyond semis; domestic infrastructure/cyber-physical stress hits GRID/CEG), AI-abundance disinflation (productivity boom that compresses incumbent margins).
2. Add duration rows (TLT, ZROZ/GOVZ) via the R1–R4 pipeline so the deflation hedge can compete for weight.
3. Attach per-row confidence grades (A: empirically corroborated, B: thesis-consistent, C: model-derived) and haircut C-grade rows by default in optimization.
4. Adopt the −200bp level stress as a standing gate: report P5/ruin under it alongside DRB40@6.
5. Keep: two-sided thematic construction, overlay documentation, robust blends.

Net effect on current conclusions: none reversed — under every stress applied the ordering P1 < P2 < R2_final ≈ R1b_final holds or widens; the review caps confidence in absolute multiples, not in the allocation choice. The likeliest R6 change is a few points migrating from equity sleeves toward duration once TLT/ZROZ rows exist and a deflation scenario can pay them.
